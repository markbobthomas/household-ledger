Which duplicate should I remove?

Household Ledger checks your saved forecast changes against each account’s unique Plaid ID—not its name. Keep the copy marked “Has saved links.” An account with saved predictions or category edits is protected from removal until those changes are reassigned. If neither copy has links, review its actual transactions before deciding. Automatic predictions from bank history are not saved edits.

Do not use “Disconnect everything” to remove a duplicate.
