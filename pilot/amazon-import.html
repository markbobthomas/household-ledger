Set up Amazon browser sync on desktop (optional)

One-time setup for Chrome or Edge on a computer. This is an experimental manual import, not automatic background syncing. The ZIP import above supports more orders.

  1. Download Ledger's extension files (ZIP) and unzip them.
  2. In desktop Chrome, open chrome://extensions (Edge: edge://extensions), enable Developer mode, select Load unpacked, then select the unzipped household-ledger-main/browser/amazon-ledger folder.
  3. Open the extension. Paste your private Ledger website address and click Connect Ledger; approve access to that Ledger site only.
  4. Sign in to Ledger and Amazon in the same browser. Open Amazon Your Orders, choose the order period, and click Sync Amazon orders in the extension.
  5. Ledger opens this import page with a CSV ready. Review the products and click Import and save. No bank purchases are created.

Only clearly priced single-item orders from up to five pages are included. Mixed orders and uncertain amounts are skipped. There is no iPad or iPhone extension support.

Details and other import options

Confirmed matches use amounts and dates and can split one posted charge among product categories. Possible and date-only matches remain clearly labeled. Existing handwritten Notes, manual category choices and all bank amounts remain untouched.

Optional desktop Chrome/Edge Amazon sync setup is above. Compatibility with a signed-in live Amazon session is not yet verified.

Saved Amazon matching quality

Counts use your current saved posted Amazon charges, not the number of products uploaded. No data is sent to AI to calculate these figures.

Checking saved matches…

Manual and AI-reviewed counts describe saved choices, not proof a category was correct. Unidentified or uncertain charges may need another Amazon export or your review. Existing specific categories are protected.
