Financial Chat

Ask questions or request a complete analysis. The AI investigates Ledger's recorded finances and predictions, chooses its own charts, and checks numerical claims against Ledger's calculations.

Exactly what can the AI access?

Ledger examines its available bank-history records, balances, saved budget targets, future transactions, expected bills, payment reconciliations, card statements and canonical three-year projected daily balances. Initial data sent to the AI includes account aliases, amounts, dates, summaries and selected individual merchant records. The AI can request further bounded searches of the complete relevant source, and can request Ledger-calculated charts. The AI does not automatically receive every individual raw record. Detailed descriptions may identify merchants. Full account numbers, original bank/transaction IDs, authentication information, bank tokens and encryption keys are not sent. Questions and returned evidence are sent to OpenAI. No financial records or predictions are changed.
