AI Financial Outlook

AI-led analysis of future balances, expected obligations and financial risks. Ledger supplies the calculations and supporting records.

Review your finances

The AI can investigate specific historical transactions, forecast events, payment cycles and daily balance windows before making its assessment. It will choose the most relevant findings and charts.

What financial information is shared?

For each analysis, Ledger first scans the connected history and canonical three-year forecast locally. The AI receives account aliases, actual/available balances, forecast lows, saved budget targets and forecasting decisions, expected bills and credit-card statements, monthly spending/merchant aggregates, and selected detailed transaction evidence. It may then request up to five targeted, read-only investigations of specific history, future payments, credit-card statements or balance windows. Ledger searches all relevant local records and sends bounded matching summaries and selected individual records to OpenAI. Individual merchant descriptions are sanitized but may remain recognizable. Full bank account numbers, original transaction IDs, Plaid credentials, passwords and API keys are not shared. Detailed source records are not universally sent or reviewed. All bank records and predictions remain unchanged.
