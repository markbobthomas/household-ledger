AI financial outlook

An open-ended review of your future balances, ability to cover scheduled payments, historical patterns and financial risks. The AI chooses what warrants attention; Ledger's own calculations remain authoritative.

Analyze your financial picture

Ledger scans its available bank history and canonical three-year forecast. For the AI review it sends temporary account aliases, current/available balances, dated forecast lows, expected payments and deposits, credit-card and payment reconciliation summaries, historical monthly/category/merchant totals, selected forecast events and up to 140 detailed recent transactions. Detailed records are sampled, not all sent. Sanitized merchant names can still be recognizable. No full account numbers, bank tokens, passwords or API credentials are sent. OpenAI receives the analysis data only after your consent below.
