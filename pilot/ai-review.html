AI Financial Analysis

One click starts an independent investigation of your full available Ledger data. The AI chooses which questions to examine, independently requests source-calculated comparisons, and builds the presentation best suited to its findings. Detailed evidence stays expandable.

What information is shared, and what can the AI change?

Ledger reviews available bank history, account aliases, dates and balances, categorized merchants, budget targets, expected payments, card obligations, reconciliations and the canonical three-year forecast. The AI receives a bounded initial evidence summary and can request more source-calculated results. It does not receive full account numbers, raw transaction IDs, account access tokens, credentials or private server files. Some sanitized descriptions and dollar amounts may identify financial activity. OpenAI receives evidence for this run, with API storage disabled. External public research queries contain only a restricted generic topic.

Automatically verified corrections can reject unsupported AI findings or fix derived analysis displays, with a per-run audit trail. Original bank records, user categories and forecast rules are not automatically modified: those require separate financial-integrity safeguards.
