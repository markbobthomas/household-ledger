What information is shared, and what can the AI change?

Ledger makes its complete normalized financial history, expected events, forecast balances, card statement obligations and derived supporting evidence available through a broad starting snapshot and a page-by-page read-only source tool. The AI decides what additional records and calculations to examine. It does not receive full account numbers, raw transaction IDs, account access tokens, credentials or private server files. Some sanitized descriptions and dollar amounts may identify financial activity. OpenAI receives evidence for this run, with API storage disabled. External public research queries contain only a restricted generic topic.

Automatically verified corrections can reject unsupported AI findings or fix derived analysis displays, with a per-run audit trail. Original bank records, user categories and forecast rules are not automatically modified: those require separate financial-integrity safeguards.
