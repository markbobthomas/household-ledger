# Ledger — early November 27, 2026 release notes (verbatim archived segment)

### 2026-11-27 — Distinguish initial bank-day adjustment from unpaid calendar-day carry-forward (source merged)

**Cause and decision:** Canonical `asOf` was treated as a completed day, so a scheduled but unposted current-day cash bill skipped to the next bank business day. Original weekend/holiday cash-out schedules should move to the preceding bank-open day, but a payment that actually remains unpaid is carried onto each subsequent **calendar** day, including weekends and holidays.  
**Code:** [PR #799](https://github.com/markbobthomas/household-ledger/pull/799), `main` merge `4fc94a08ce6d8736b5a69430439cfce4d0047f62`. Keep today's canonical forecast rows in the calendar, include today's cash movement in projected closing balance while leaving posted bank opening cash untouched, and roll unposted previous-day obligations onto the new snapshot date (not next bank business day). Apply to recurring payments, user one-offs and card settlement plans, and preserve bank-posting one-to-one matches, pending reserve safeguards, fixed recurrence anchors and true overdue alerts.  
**Checks/deployment:** Final PR head `abb687c35bbcde442435cb73d83b6e10e7449bab` passed [banking and forecast CI #46008559054](https://github.com/markbobthomas/household-ledger/actions/runs/46008559054) and [desktop Chromium/iPhone WebKit CI #46008559155](https://github.com/markbobthomas/household-ledger/actions/runs/46008559155). Fictional fixtures cover Friday due-today, Saturday and Sunday missed roll-forward, holiday initial shift, posted/pending matching, card payments, multiple missed installments and projection conservation. **Private Render live SHA and household Plaid output are still unverified**; merge does not prove private deployment.

### 2026-11-27 — Universal early-posted cash vs prediction reconciliation (PR #802)

**Problem:** A bank-observed checking deposit posted before its forecast payday could still appear as an additional predicted deposit, inflating the projected balance. The fix must be general across bank cash movements, not a paycheck-specific exception.  
**Code:** [PR #802](https://github.com/markbobthomas/household-ledger/pull/802) enhances the existing canonical one-to-one settled-versus-forecast pass for cash-direction equivalence across incoming credits/refunds, uncategorized ACH credits, outgoing bills and paired linked movements. Name changes are not blocking when account, direction, near date and amount jointly prove a unique match. Do not borrow unrelated or paired bank transfers, erase a second alternating payroll stream, or treat pending deposits as already posted. Preserve original mortgage due-today/calendar-day roll-forward and recurring/holiday anchors.  
**Safety/checks:** Fictional multi-type regression fixtures and same-day mortgage/paycheck integration; banking/forecast CI [#46010817813](https://github.com/markbobthomas/household-ledger/actions/runs/46010817813) passed on code head `b383755c94af165cc78b5363c160308c0c63b2a9`. No Plaid/bank facts, saved financial preferences, auth, infrastructure or private files changed. **Live private Render SHA and production reconciliation remain unverified**; separately verify existing deployment and bank-sync freshness before claiming user-visible resolution.

### 2026-11-27 — Pending early income already in bank available (PR #803)

**Cause:** The private app was verified running the preceding release, yet a deposited-early paycheck still appeared in the payday forecast. A pending bank credit may increase *available* while *current* remains unchanged. Prior rules excluded every pending incoming credit, then incorrectly forecast the same money again from an available-balance starting point. No private banking details or screenshots are committed.  
**Change:** [PR #803](https://github.com/markbobthomas/household-ledger/pull/803) checks same-account pending credit/debit totals against bank available-minus-current. If the complete pending balance effect is verified and a unique predicted income/refund matches, do not forecast that cash again; keep transaction `pending` and flag `includedInAvailable`. Handle simultaneously reserved pending debits, preserve alternating payroll and due-today mortgage, and send ambiguous matches to Review. Uncertain cash evidence does not delete a forecast. Bank facts and settings untouched.  
**Evidence/checks:** The new fictional regression failed first on old code ([banking CI #46013334664](https://github.com/markbobthomas/household-ledger/actions/runs/46013334664)), then expanded pending-income/refund, net-debit, inferred payroll, wrong-account and ambiguity cases passed [banking CI #46013632131](https://github.com/markbobthomas/household-ledger/actions/runs/46013632131), feature code `1411ce4e26b49e49ebf2706f7a3a1eb0140e8724`.
