Match Amazon items to existing bank charges. Never adds purchases.

Automatic order sync

When enabled, checks Amazon about every 6 hours while this desktop browser is running. Saves item Notes and confident categories directly to Ledger. Uncertain categories stay for review.

Automatic sync is off.

Uses your existing Amazon and Ledger sign-ins. No passwords are saved in the extension. It may ask you to sign in again if either session expires.

Manual import (optional)

Open Amazon Your Orders in this browser, then load a review-only CSV into Ledger.

Currently scans up to five pages of clearly priced single-item orders; mixed orders and refunds still need ZIP import. Not compatible with iPad/iPhone.
