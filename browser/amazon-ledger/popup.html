Amazon → Ledger

1. Sign in to your private Ledger in this browser, paste its website address below, then Connect Ledger once.

2. Open Amazon Your Orders in this browser while signed in. Choose an order period.

3. Review the CSV opened in Ledger, then click Import and save there.

Experimental, desktop Chrome/Edge only. Reads up to five pages of clearly priced single-item orders. Multi-item and uncertain orders are skipped. No passwords, cookies or order history are stored. The ZIP/CSV importer remains available.
